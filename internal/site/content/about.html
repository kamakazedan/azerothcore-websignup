What's running on {{REALM}}

Wrath of the Lich King {{GAME_VERSION}} on AzerothCore, in a world that's never empty: AI-controlled players quest, level, run dungeons and chat alongside the real ones.

Playerbots
Invite bots you meet into your group, or make your own, and adventure with them.
Bots that talk
Bots chat in General, Trade, /say, party and guild chat, answer you, and joke among themselves in the cities. Their lines come from an AI language model on the server's own hardware, so they can be wrong or silly. They're bots: don't tell them anything personal.
Dungeon Clear
With a bot tank in your group, the bots run the dungeon while you heal or deal damage. Talk to them as you would a normal group.
Group XP
Real players grouped together don't split the experience from kills.
A stocked Auction House
An auction bot lists goods and buys them, so there's always something for sale.
Area looting
One click loots every corpse nearby. Type .aoeloot off if you'd rather loot one at a time.
Quest items for the whole party
A quest item drops for everyone in the group who needs it, not just one of you.
