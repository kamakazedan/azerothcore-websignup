Playing with bots

Bots in your group answer to these, typed in party chat in lowercase. The ones that start with a dot work in any chat box.

Party chat commands

follow and stay
Follow you, or hold where they are.
attack
Attack your target.
flee
Drop the fight and follow you.
summon
Teleport to you. It's the fix for a bot that's stuck or lost.
drink
Casters low on mana sit down and drink.
release and revive
Release a dead bot's spirit, then use the spirit healer.
@heal follow
Start with @tank, @heal or @dps to talk to just those bots.

Careful with “go”. On its own in party chat, go is a bot command: every bot runs at the nearest creature. Say lets go instead.

Dungeon Clear

ready or .dc on
Starts the run once you're inside. You need a bot tank in the group, and you heal or do damage. The tank leads the way.
mb, wait or brb
Pauses the run, and ready carries on. You can ask the group what's next, too.
.dc pause
Pauses or resumes the run.
.dc skip
Skips something the bots are stuck on.
.dc off
Stops the run. Use it to tank yourself: the bots follow you and fight what you pull.

Bots stuck or lost in a dungeon? Type .dc pause, then summon and follow in party chat, then .dc pause again to carry on.
